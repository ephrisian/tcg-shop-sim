TCG Shop Simulator — Feature Outline
====================================

Purpose
-------
This outline organizes proposed functionality by implementation dependency. It
describes future features, not commitments to a final design. Product and
balance questions are listed at the end and should be answered before the
affected feature is implemented.

Current baseline
----------------
- The game currently has a single city/store list, sealed-product purchasing
  and opening, a basic live-show toggle with viewer types, one flat binder
  collection, storage units, and a player-facing Data page for Lorcast imports.
- Card collection editing is explored separately in card_collector.js, but
  there is not yet a shared, production-ready source-data service.
- The save model stores cards in desk, storage, or one binder collection. New
  inventory and location features will need an explicit, migrated data model.

Feature outline
===============

1. Card data platform and independent collection editor
-------------------------------------------------------
- Define a canonical schema for games/products, sets, cards, product variants,
  images/art, pack composition, and special attributes. Preserve original
  provider data and stable source identifiers.
- Create a separate source-data editor for adding/editing sets and cards,
  validating records, previewing art, and importing/exporting set data.
- Integrate provider adapters where APIs exist. For providers without APIs,
  support a documented manual or file-based workflow. Evaluate
  card_collector.js as prior art, not as a finished editor or backend.
- Configure pack and booster-box art, card set membership, product contents,
  and set-specific redemption definitions as data rather than screen logic.
- Choose how set data is stored, versioned, backed up, and delivered to the
  game. Remove source-data management from the player-facing game once an
  independent editor/replacement workflow is available.

2. Inventory, storage, and personal collection foundations
----------------------------------------------------------
- Consolidate Shop, Sealed, and Storage under an Inventory destination.
- Keep personal collecting and desk work clearly separated from inventory
  offered for sale. Decide whether Collection/Binder is its own main tab or a
  view nested under Inventory.
- Define card-instance ownership and location (desk, a specific storage
  drawer, binder, or sellable stock) so moving, selling, searching, or
  fulfilling an order cannot duplicate or silently lose a card.
- Support multiple named binders. Binder variants have capacities, page
  counts, designs, themes, prices, shop availability, and used resale values.
- Let players buy binders from shops, name them, place/reorder cards, empty
  them, and sell them. On emptying, cards not moved elsewhere go to storage;
  define behavior when storage has no room.
- Show two 3-by-3 binder pages at once. Support drag-to-slot placement with a
  clear satisfying snap/placement affordance.
- Show how many copies of a card the player already owns during binder
  selection. Show the full set checklist, using mystery/hidden cards for
  cards not yet obtained.
- Make drawer identity and capacity explicit. Map desk piles to destination
  drawers and provide clear capacity/overflow behavior.
- Improve desk actions so after the player acts on a selected card, the next
  card appears without an extra tap.
- Support sales from eligible inventory as a way to fund additional sealed
  purchases.

3. Live Rip Ship orders and card search
---------------------------------------
- Extend the existing live-show toggle/viewer counts into a queue of buyer
  requests, with statuses, fulfillment, decline, rewards, and session state.
- Before starting a live, let the player choose sellable sealed products and
  eligible cards. Decide how stored cards and binder cards are listed and
  whether binder cards require explicit opt-in.
- Generate requests ranging from vague criteria (for example, a character
  or rarity) to a specific card, using the selected sellable stock and
  supported set data.
- For card requests, let the player select a drawer and search a card-number
  range. Charge energy based on range size and repeated/narrowing attempts.
  Use the example costs (2 energy per 100 cards; 12 for a whole drawer) only
  after confirming whether those are desired defaults.
- Allow a player to fulfill or decline. Track declines and define how they
  affect foot traffic, reputation, future order volume, and buyer satisfaction.
- Define fulfillment results: card ownership/location updates, revenue,
  customer satisfaction, and live metrics.
- Decide what happens when a search misses, when stock is unavailable, when a
  live is ended with requests pending, at day end, or after a browser reload.

4. World map, neighborhoods, cities, and travel
-----------------------------------------------
- Replace the flat store list with a configurable hierarchy of regions,
  cities, neighborhoods/areas, and shops.
- Start in one city with Uptown, Downtown, City Center, Suburbs, and SODO.
- Add Portlandia, SeaTown, MidWesteria, ShyTown, and AppleShire as locked
  cities, with unlock rules to be defined.
- Distinguish travel between cities from visiting a neighborhood/shop.
  Display and charge the appropriate money and energy costs.
- Associate local stores, stock, products, and binder offerings with their
  locations. Keep location definitions out of screen components.
- Establish whether the player initially visits stores only or can eventually
  own/run shops across the fictional country.

5. Online commerce, shipping, and progression
---------------------------------------------
- Add online shops as an alternative to physical visits.
- Purchases arrive after a configurable number of in-game days. Offer
  expedited shipping at additional cost and show shipment status/arrival day.
- Integrate sales revenue, purchasing, shipping, travel, energy, live rewards,
  and foot traffic into one configurable economy.
- Define the initial online vendors/products, stock rules, delivery timing,
  expedited fees, and handling of multiple shipments and save/load.

6. Multi-game and collectible-product support
---------------------------------------------
- Use the shared schema and import/editor workflow to support Lorcana,
  Riftbound, Pokemon, Magic, and collectible-card products from Topps,
  Card.Fun, Kakawow, and additional sources.
- Normalize provider-specific rarities, card types, IDs, images, prices, and
  game-specific attributes without losing source metadata.
- Make product art, product-to-set mapping, and pack contents configurable by
  game/set. Keep pack-generation rules data-driven.
- Scope card identity and set completion by game/provider/set so duplicate
  names or provider IDs do not collide.
- Prioritize an initial supported game/source and provide test data before
  broadening import coverage.

7. Set redemptions and milestone rewards
----------------------------------------
- Define set completion/milestone requirements and reward types in set data.
  Rewards may include binders or special cards.
- Show eligibility and progress in the relevant set/collection views.
- Prevent duplicate claims unless a redemption is explicitly repeatable.
- Add claimed rewards to inventory/collection and support authoring and
  validation in the separate source-data editor.

Cross-cutting implementation considerations
===========================================
- Add versioned save migrations before replacing the current flat binder,
  storage, and sealed-product structures. Preserve existing saves and provide
  a recoverable path if migration fails.
- Keep runtime game and source-data authoring separate, while sharing the
  canonical schema and validation logic where appropriate.
- Keep tunable economy values, search-energy formulas, unlock rules, live
  request generation, product contents, and redemption definitions out of
  screen components.
- Resolve browser configuration delivery before adding developer settings:
  the browser game cannot assume it can read a developer-local settings.ini.
  Choose a build-time configuration, fetched static file, or editor-managed
  configuration.
- Design empty/loading/error/invalid-data states, insufficient funds/energy,
  no storage capacity, interrupted lives, and failed imports.
- Add focused tests for save migrations, card movement/ownership invariants,
  search energy, request resolution, shipping timing, and redemption claims.
- Retain source attribution, data provenance, and image-use permission
  information where required.

Questions to resolve before implementation
==========================================

Scope and sequencing
--------------------
- Is personal Collection/Binder a separate main tab, or nested within
  Inventory?
- Should features ship in increments that leave the current game playable?
  Which milestone is the first user-facing release?
- Which game/card source is supported first, and what initial set data is
  available for testing?

Inventory, storage, and binders
-------------------------------
- Are binder cards eligible for live sale/search by default, opt-in per card,
  or unavailable until removed from a binder?
- Can players create a binder during card movement, or only through shops?
- What binder capacities, page counts, purchase prices, designs, and used
  resale formula should apply?
- What happens after a card search misses: can the player continue searching,
  and does each attempt keep its energy cost?
- Is a storage pile a temporary desk grouping assigned to a drawer, or a
  persistent named group within storage?
- What happens when emptying a binder or sorting desk cards would exceed
  available storage capacity?

Live orders and economy
-----------------------
- What may a buyer request (card name, character, rarity, set, sealed
  product, or combinations), and how vague can a request be?
- How are order quantity, queue length, order timers, prices/rewards, and
  buyer satisfaction determined?
- What counts as "too many" declines, over what time window, and how large
  is the foot-traffic penalty and recovery?
- Do active requests persist across reloads, continue while away, or pause/end
  at day end?
- Is live fulfillment paid at a fixed order price, market value, negotiated
  value, or another formula?

Search and balance
------------------
- Is energy charged once per chosen range, per search attempt, or cumulatively
  as the player narrows a search? How do repeated/overlapping ranges work?
- Are the example costs (2 energy per 100 cards and 12 per full drawer) exact
  defaults or illustrative starting points?
- Is storage position stable and searchable, or does the game abstract the
  physical position of cards when drawers are sorted?

World and online commerce
-------------------------
- What are unlock conditions and travel costs for the named cities and
  neighborhoods? Does travel cost in-game time/day in addition to energy and
  money?
- Can the player own shops in multiple cities, or only visit them initially?
- Which online vendors/products are in scope? How are stock, normal/expedited
  delivery durations, and shipping fees determined?
- Can multiple shipments be in transit together? What happens if a save is
  loaded after a shipment's arrival day?

Data, configuration, and redemptions
------------------------------------
- Should developer-tunable settings use static JSON, a build-time .ini, or an
  editor that exports configuration? Who can change production values?
- Where will source set data live (bundled files, user imports, remote
  hosting, or a combination), and which import formats are required?
- For providers without APIs, is manual entry enough, or are spreadsheet/CSV
  and image-folder imports also needed?
- What source attribution, image-use permissions, and provenance must be
  recorded?
- Are pack/box art and set contents chosen by the editor, the player, or both?
- Which redemption conditions/rewards are needed first? Can redemptions be
  repeated, traded, or sold?
